FAQ

Common questions.

What is Web4Agent?

Web4Agent is a configurable trading-bot platform where users can select bot capacity, define trading parameters and manage bot workflows.

How many bots can I use?

The planned limits are 1, 5 or 10 bots depending on the selected weekly plan.

What happens when a subscription expires?

In the production system, execution should be disabled and the dashboard should present the verified reactivation flow.

How much $W4A is required?

The intended requirement is $100 worth of $W4A, calculated from an approved live token price once the production token configuration is complete.

Does Web4Agent guarantee profits?

No. Web4Agent does not guarantee profits or specific trading results. Trading involves substantial risk.
